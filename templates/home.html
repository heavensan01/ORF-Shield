Common warning signs


  • Asks for a registration, training or security fee
  • Interview only on WhatsApp or Telegram
  • Recruiter uses a Gmail / Yahoo address
  • "No experience needed, earn ₹5,000 a day"
  • Requests Aadhaar, bank or card details early

Signs of a genuine posting


  • Clear role, responsibilities and requirements
  • Official company domain and careers page
  • Structured interview process
  • No payment is ever requested from you
  • Verifiable company presence and reviews
