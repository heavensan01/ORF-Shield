How ORF Shield works

From raw job postings to a real-time fraud verdict.

1 · Data & pre-processing

Job postings from multiple sources are merged, cleaned, de-duplicated and columns renamed to text and label.

2 · Class balancing

Fake postings are rare. The majority class is down-sampled and the minority class is balanced before fine-tuning.

3 · ALBERT fine-tuning

albert-base-v1 is fine-tuned for binary classification and evaluated with accuracy, precision, recall and F1.


Tech stack


  • Python · Flask · Flask-WTF (CSRF)
  • Hugging Face Transformers · PyTorch
  • scikit-learn · pandas
  • SQLite for users & prediction history

Modules
